### Surfaces

#### Surface-0 (Page)
- No shadow
- Max-width containers inside
- Grid foundation

#### Surface-1 (Cards)
- Padding: medium
- Border: subtle
- Radius: medium

#### Surface-2 (Hover / Raised Cards)
- Shadow: soft
- Slight scale on hover (optional)
- Stronger border contrast

#### Surface-3 (Modals)
- Fixed positioning context
- Backdrop blur
- Highest elevation
